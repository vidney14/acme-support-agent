# Plans, Pricing and Support Tiers

AcmeCloud offers four plans. Every plan includes file sync across desktop, web and mobile, link sharing, and end-to-end encrypted transfer.

## Free plan

- Price: $0
- Storage: 5 GB
- Version history: 30 days
- Maximum file size: 2 GB
- Support: community forum only. Free accounts do not get ticket-based support response times.

## Pro plan

- Price: $12 per month, or $120 per year (two months free)
- Storage: 1 TB
- Version history: 180 days
- Maximum file size: 50 GB
- Support: email support with a first response within 48 hours

## Business plan

- Price: $25 per user per month, or $240 per user per year
- Minimum: 3 users
- Storage: 5 TB per user, pooled across the team
- Version history: 1 year
- Maximum file size: 50 GB
- Admin console, audit logs and SAML single sign-on (SSO)
- Support: chat and email with a first response within 24 hours, and within 4 hours for urgent (P1) issues

## Enterprise plan

- Price: custom annual contract (12 or 36 months)
- Storage: unlimited, subject to fair use
- Version history: unlimited
- Maximum file size: 50 GB
- Everything in Business, plus data residency options, a 99.95% uptime SLA and a dedicated account manager
- Support: 24/7 phone, chat and email. P1 issues get a first response within 1 hour.

## Changing plans

Customers can upgrade or downgrade from Settings → Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Before downgrading, storage use must be below the new plan's limit; otherwise sync pauses until files are removed.

Storage add-ons are available on Pro and Business: 1 TB for $40 per month.
