# Refund Policy

AcmeCloud wants customers to pay only for service they actually want. This policy explains when a charge can be refunded, who can approve it, and how long it takes.

## How refunds are paid out

Approved refunds always go back to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear on the customer's statement, depending on the bank. AcmeCloud cannot refund to a different card, to a bank account that was not used for the payment, or as cash, cryptocurrency or gift cards.

Every approved refund gets a refund reference number (format `RF-xxxxx`). Customers should keep this number if they need to follow up with their bank.

## Free plan

<!-- tiers: free -->

The Free plan is never charged, so there is nothing to refund. If a Free customer reports a charge from AcmeCloud, it usually means a paid plan was started by mistake on another account. Escalate these reports to the billing team so they can locate the account.

## Pro plan refunds

<!-- tiers: pro -->

### Pro monthly

Pro monthly charges ($12 per month) can be refunded in full if the refund is requested within 14 days of the charge date. After 14 days the charge is not refundable, but the customer can cancel so they are not charged again.

### Pro annual

Pro annual charges ($120 per year) can be refunded in full if the refund is requested within 30 days of the charge date. After 30 days, annual charges are not refundable. The customer can still turn off auto-renewal so the plan does not renew next year.

### Automated approval limit for Pro

Automated support (the AI assistant) can approve a single Pro refund of up to $50 without human review. Any Pro refund above $50, for example a full annual refund, must be approved by the billing team. In that case the assistant creates an escalation ticket with priority P2 and the billing team responds within 48 hours.

## Business plan refunds

<!-- tiers: business -->

Business charges can be refunded if the refund is requested within 30 days of the charge date. This applies to both monthly and annual Business billing.

Automated support can approve a single Business refund of up to $200 without human review. Refunds above $200 require billing team approval through a P2 escalation ticket. The billing team responds to Business escalations within 24 hours.

### Seat reductions

When a Business admin removes seats in the middle of a billing period, the unused time is credited to the account as a prorated account credit. Seat reductions are not refunded to the payment method. The credit is applied automatically to the next invoice.

## Enterprise refunds and credits

<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise billing, refunds and credits are governed by the customer's Master Services Agreement (MSA). Automated support cannot issue refunds on Enterprise accounts. All Enterprise refund requests are routed to the customer's dedicated account manager through an escalation ticket.

Enterprise customers who experienced downtime should read the Enterprise SLA policy. Downtime is compensated with service credits, not refunds.

## Rules that apply to every refund

- **One automated refund per 90 days.** Automated support can approve at most one refund per account in any rolling 90-day period. A second request within 90 days must be reviewed by the billing team.
- **Past-due accounts.** Accounts with a past-due balance are not eligible for refunds until the outstanding balance is paid.
- **No double refunds.** An invoice that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again.
- **Partial refunds.** A partial refund can be issued for any amount up to the invoice total.
- **Duplicate charges.** If the customer was charged twice for the same period, the duplicate charge is always refundable in full, even outside the normal refund window. The automated approval limits still apply.
- **Identity first.** Refunds can only be discussed in detail and issued after the customer's identity has been verified.
