# Billing, Payments and Invoices

## Billing dates

Monthly plans are charged on the same day each month as the original signup date. Annual plans are charged once a year on the renewal date. AcmeCloud sends a reminder email 7 days before every annual renewal.

## Failed payments and past-due accounts

If a payment fails, AcmeCloud retries the card 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, the account status becomes **past due**.

A past-due account keeps full access during a 14-day grace period. After the grace period ends, the account becomes read-only: files can be viewed and downloaded but not uploaded or edited. Paying the outstanding balance restores full access immediately.

Past-due accounts are not eligible for refunds until the balance is paid.

## Updating a payment method

Payment methods can only be updated in the Billing Portal (Settings → Billing → Payment method). For security reasons, support agents, including the AI assistant, never accept card numbers or bank details in chat, email or over the phone. If a customer tries to share card details, the agent must stop them and point them to the Billing Portal.

## Invoices and receipts

All invoices are available in Settings → Billing → Invoices and can be downloaded as PDF. Business and Enterprise admins can add a company name, billing address and VAT or GST ID, which appear on all future invoices. Past invoices cannot be re-issued with a different company name.

## Price changes

AcmeCloud gives at least 30 days' notice by email before any price change takes effect on an existing subscription.

## Taxes

Prices exclude sales tax, VAT and GST. Tax is calculated from the billing address and shown on the invoice.
