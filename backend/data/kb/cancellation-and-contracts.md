# Cancellation, Renewals and Contract Status

## Cancelling a monthly plan

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time from Settings → Plan → Cancel subscription. The plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, and the customer is not charged again. Partial months are not refunded, except as described in the Refund Policy.

## Cancelling an annual plan

Annual plans renew automatically. Customers can turn off auto-renewal at any time. The plan stays active until the end of the paid year. Annual charges are refundable only within the window described in the Refund Policy (30 days for Pro and Business).

## Enterprise contracts

<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise contracts run for 12 or 36 months and renew automatically for the same term. To stop a renewal, the customer must give written notice at least 60 days before the renewal date. Notice must come from an authorised contact named in the contract and is handled by the account manager. Automated support cannot cancel or change Enterprise contracts.

## Contract and subscription status

Every paid account has one of these statuses:

- **Active**: the subscription is paid and in good standing.
- **Past due**: a payment failed. See Billing, Payments and Invoices for the 14-day grace period.
- **Expired**: the subscription or contract ended without renewal. The account becomes read-only for 30 days so the customer can download their files. After 30 days the account is downgraded to Free, and files above the Free storage limit are scheduled for deletion 90 days after expiry.
- **Cancelled**: the customer cancelled and the paid period has ended. Same data rules as Expired.

## Reactivating an expired account

An expired or cancelled account can be reactivated at any time before data deletion by choosing a plan in Settings → Plan. Reactivation restores full access immediately. Files that were already deleted cannot be recovered.
