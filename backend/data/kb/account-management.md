# Account Settings Support Can Change

## Fields the assistant can update

After the customer's identity is verified, the AI assistant can update these profile fields directly:

- display name
- phone number
- preferred contact channel (email, phone or chat)
- time zone
- marketing email preference (opt in or opt out)

Changes take effect immediately and the customer receives a confirmation email.

## Changes the customer must make themselves

These changes require extra security checks and cannot be made by the assistant:

- **Sign-in email address**: changed in Settings → Account → Email. AcmeCloud sends a confirmation link to both the old and new address.
- **Payment method**: changed only in the Billing Portal. Agents never accept card details.
- **Plan changes**: upgrades and downgrades are self-service in Settings → Plan.
- **Password and 2FA**: see Sign-in, Passwords and Two-Factor Authentication.

## Changes that need a human

- **Transferring account ownership** on Business and Enterprise accounts requires a request from the current owner or an admin and is handled by the support team through an escalation ticket.
- **Merging two accounts** is not supported. Files can be moved by sharing them from one account to the other.
