# Data Export, Deletion and Privacy Requests

## Exporting your data

Customers can export all of their files and metadata from Settings → Data → Export. AcmeCloud prepares a ZIP archive within 24 hours and emails a download link. The link is valid for 7 days. Exports are available on every plan, including Free and expired accounts in their read-only period.

## Deleting an account

Account owners can delete their account from Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion has a 30-day grace period during which the owner can cancel it by signing in. After 30 days, all files and metadata are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

## Privacy requests (GDPR and CCPA)

Requests to access, correct or erase personal data under GDPR, CCPA or similar laws are handled by the privacy team within 30 days. Customers can email privacy@acmecloud.example, or the assistant can create an escalation ticket in the privacy category. The assistant cannot fulfil privacy requests itself.

## Data retention after a plan ends

- Expired or cancelled paid accounts: read-only for 30 days, then files above the Free limit are scheduled for deletion 90 days after expiry.
- Free accounts with no sign-in for 12 months receive a warning email and are deleted 30 days later if there is still no sign-in.

## Where data is stored

Data for Free, Pro and Business accounts is stored in the United States. Enterprise customers can choose data residency in the US, the EU or Australia as part of their contract.
