# Escalation and Human Handoff

The AI assistant resolves what it can and hands everything else to a human through an escalation ticket. A good handoff means the customer never has to repeat themselves.

## When the assistant must escalate

- A refund is above the automated approval limit, or any refund on an Enterprise account.
- The customer's identity cannot be verified.
- Security concerns: suspected account compromise, or a lost 2FA device without recovery codes.
- Privacy or legal requests (GDPR, CCPA, subpoenas).
- A technical issue that continues after the troubleshooting steps in the knowledge base.
- The question is not covered by the knowledge base, so the assistant cannot give a reliable answer.
- The customer asks for a human.

## Ticket priorities

- **P1**: security incident, account compromise, or the service is completely unavailable for the customer.
- **P2**: billing disputes, refund approvals, Enterprise contract questions.
- **P3**: general technical problems.
- **P4**: feedback and feature requests.

## Human response targets by plan

| Plan | Standard | P1 |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Best effort, up to 5 business days | Best effort |
| Pro | 48 hours | 24 hours |
| Business | 24 hours | 4 hours |
| Enterprise | Per SLA severity table | 1 hour, 24/7 |

## What a ticket must include

Every escalation ticket includes a short summary of the problem, what the assistant already tried, the customer's plan and account ID (if verified), and the priority. The customer receives the ticket number so they can reference it.
