# Enterprise Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This SLA applies only to customers on the Enterprise plan with an active contract.

## Uptime commitment

AcmeCloud commits to 99.95% monthly uptime for file access and sync. Uptime is measured per calendar month by AcmeCloud's external monitoring.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, the customer is eligible for a service credit, calculated as a percentage of that month's fee:

| Monthly uptime | Service credit |
|---|---|
| Below 99.95% but at least 99.0% | 10% |
| Below 99.0% but at least 95.0% | 25% |
| Below 95.0% | 50% |

Service credits are applied to the next invoice. They are not paid out as cash refunds.

## How to claim a credit

The customer must submit a credit claim within 30 days of the end of the affected month. Claims are submitted through the dedicated account manager. Automated support can open an escalation ticket for the claim, but cannot approve or calculate the final credit.

## Exclusions

Downtime does not count against the SLA when it is caused by:

- scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance
- the customer's own network, devices or integrations
- events outside AcmeCloud's reasonable control (force majeure)

## Incident severity and response times

| Severity | Definition | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Service unavailable or data at risk for many users | 1 hour, 24/7 |
| P2 | Major feature degraded, no workaround | 4 hours |
| P3 | Minor issue or workaround available | 1 business day |
| P4 | Question or feature request | 2 business days |
